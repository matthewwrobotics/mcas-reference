## Mechanism of action

Fexofenadine is a second-generation H1 receptor antagonist and the active
metabolite of terfenadine, which was withdrawn over cardiac effects that
fexofenadine does not share. It blocks the H1 receptor, acting after mast-cell
mediators reach it.

It crosses into the central nervous system less readily than the first
generation, which is the basis of the class's lower-sedation profile. Its
consumer label differs from the rest of the class in one practical respect: it
directs that the product not be taken with fruit juice, and that antacids
containing aluminium or magnesium be separated from it, because both reduce how
much drug is absorbed.
