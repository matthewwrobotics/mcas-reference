## Mechanism of action

Desloratadine is the active metabolite of loratadine and a second-generation H1
receptor antagonist. It blocks the H1 receptor downstream of mediator release.

It is the only member of this class listed here that is dispensed on
prescription in the United States rather than over the counter. That difference
is regulatory: the others reached over-the-counter status through the FDA's
switch process, and this one has not. Nothing in the evidence cited here
distinguishes it clinically from the rest of the class.
