Ketotifen acts at the histamine H1 receptor as an antagonist, and is additionally described in the mast cell stabiliser literature as inhibiting mediator release from mast cells. It is one of several compounds developed for another indication whose stabilising activity was identified subsequently, and reviews of the class group it with drugs whose stabilising mechanism is less fully characterised than their primary receptor action.

Because it carries both activities, ketotifen appears in treatment discussions as a single agent covering two steps that would otherwise require two drugs.
