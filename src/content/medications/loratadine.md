## Mechanism of action

Loratadine is a second-generation H1 receptor antagonist. It blocks the H1
receptor after mast-cell mediators have been released, and penetrates the
central nervous system less than first-generation agents.

It is metabolised to desloratadine, which is separately marketed and has its own
entry. That relationship is why the two behave similarly, and why a reader who
has tried one may find the other described in the same terms.
