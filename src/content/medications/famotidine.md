Famotidine antagonises the histamine H2 receptor. H2 receptors are expressed on gastric parietal cells, where they drive acid secretion, and also on vascular smooth muscle and on several immune cell populations — which is why H2 blockade is discussed in mast cell disease as covering histamine effects that H1 blockade leaves untouched.

In the stepwise mediator-blockade approach, H2 antihistamines are the conventional second step after H1 antihistamines.
