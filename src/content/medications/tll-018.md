TLL-018 is an oral small molecule designed to inhibit tyrosine kinase 2 and Janus kinase 1. Those kinases transmit signals from several cytokine receptors and are used across multiple immune-cell types. The mechanism is therefore broader than blocking a released mast-cell mediator and less mast-cell-specific than anti-IgE, BTK, or KIT targeting.

A phase Ib pilot randomized 41 people with chronic spontaneous urticaria to TLL-018 or placebo for the initial comparison. The publication reports urticaria endpoints and treatment-emergent laboratory abnormalities, but it does not report a mast-cell measurement. That distinction is why this page carries the related-inflammatory-condition tag and a separate “no mast cell evidence” grade despite the randomized study design.

The phase 3 CSU study is active but no longer recruiting. Its registry currently records actual enrolment while its planned completion remains in the future. Those facts make the program visible as investigational; they do not supply phase 3 results. Searches of PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov located no direct MCAS or mastocytosis study for TLL-018.
