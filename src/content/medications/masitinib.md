Masitinib inhibits KIT along with the LYN and FYN kinases that sit downstream of the high-affinity IgE receptor, giving it a proposed action on mast cell activation as well as on proliferation. Unlike avapritinib it is not selective for the D816V mutant, which is the pharmacologic reason its activity in D816V-positive disease has been questioned.

Its trial status is the reason this site takes statuses from the registry and the sponsor only. The Phase 3 record NCT04333108 is currently carried on ClinicalTrials.gov as **status unknown** — the registry's own flag for a study whose sponsor has not updated the record past its expected completion date. Secondary trackers frequently continue to display an earlier "ongoing" or "recruiting" status in this situation, with no indication that the underlying record has gone quiet.
