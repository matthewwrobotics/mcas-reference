## Mechanism of action

Levocetirizine is the active enantiomer of cetirizine — the half of the racemic
mixture that binds the H1 receptor. Cetirizine sold as a racemate contains it
already. Like the rest of the class it blocks the H1 receptor, acting after
mast-cell mediators have been released rather than preventing their release.

Its separate existence is a regulatory and commercial fact more than a
mechanistic one. It is listed here because a reader looking for Xyzal should
find something, and because its own label — not cetirizine's — is what governs
how it is sold.
