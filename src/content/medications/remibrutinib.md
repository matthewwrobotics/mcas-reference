Remibrutinib is a small-molecule inhibitor of Bruton tyrosine kinase (BTK), an intracellular signalling protein used by FcεRI and other immune receptors. The FDA label identifies BTK in mast cells, basophils, B cells, macrophages, and thrombocytes, and notes activity against the related kinases TEC and BMX.

In laboratory work, researchers exposed CD34-derived human mast cells and blood basophils to IgE cross-linking or serum from people with chronic urticaria. Remibrutinib reduced the activation marker CD63 under those experimental conditions. That experiment demonstrates a mast-cell mechanism in culture; it does not establish an MCAS outcome.

Two randomised, placebo-controlled phase 3 trials were published in chronic spontaneous urticaria, and the current US label carries that indication for adults who remain symptomatic despite H1-antihistamine treatment. The label explicitly limits the indication to chronic spontaneous urticaria rather than other forms of urticaria, and it does not include MCAS.
