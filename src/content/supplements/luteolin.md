Luteolin is a flavonoid found in a range of vegetables and fruits. The polyphenol literature reports that it suppresses production of interleukin-6, interleukin-8, and vascular endothelial growth factor from tumour necrosis factor-stimulated keratinocytes, and groups it with quercetin as a polyphenol acting on mast cell signalling.

As with quercetin, the evidence base is laboratory work rather than human outcome data in mast cell activation syndrome, and it is listed here on mechanistic grounds only.
